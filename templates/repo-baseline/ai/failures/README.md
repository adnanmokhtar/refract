# ai/failures/

The "don't-retry" knowledge. Approaches that were tried in this repo and failed — captured so future agents don't repeat them.

## What goes here

- `_index.md` — the failure catalog. One entry per failed approach: what was tried, why it failed, what to do instead, when.

## Who writes here

- `execute-plan.md` and `verify-plan.md` (on PLAN VIOLATED) — record the dead end.
- `learn-from-task.md` — the failed-approach rung of the persistence pyramid.

## Distinction

- `failures/` — what NOT to do (and why it failed before).
- `decisions/` — why we chose what we chose (with alternatives).
- `patterns/` — how to do things that work.
- `dynamic/learnings.md` — raw, unproven observations + corrections.

## Empty?

`_index.md` ships with a header so the catalog can be appended immediately. Read it before planning work in an area you haven't touched.
