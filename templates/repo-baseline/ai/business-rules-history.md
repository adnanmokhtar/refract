# Business rules — history (append only)

Every change to a business rule, oldest first. `ai/business-rules.md` holds the rules as they are
today; this file holds how they got there: who changed what, when, and why. Lines are never edited
or removed. A rule reversed later gets a new line.

## Format

```
<YYYY-MM-DD> · added|changed|removed · <area> · <rule before> → <rule after> · <why> · <card> · <PR>
```

For an added rule, `<rule before>` is `—`. For a removed rule, `<rule after>` is `—`.
