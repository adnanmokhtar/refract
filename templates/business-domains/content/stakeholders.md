# Content — stakeholders

Content platforms span creators, editors, operators, readers, and advertising/partner ecosystems. Each has different power, different tools, different KPIs.

## Reader / audience

The reason anything happens.

**Workflows:**
- Discover content (direct, search, social, newsletter).
- Read article.
- Share / react / comment.
- Subscribe to newsletter.
- Return (ideally repeatedly).

**Pain points:**
- Slow-loading pages.
- Intrusive ads / popups.
- Paywall mid-article without warning.
- Broken comments / cluttered.
- Subscription fatigue (too many newsletter asks).
- Can't find previously-read article.
- Headlines that don't match content (clickbait).

**KPIs:**
- Session duration.
- Pages per session.
- Return rate.
- Newsletter subscription.
- Share rate.

## Commenter

Subset of reader; active participant.

**Pain points:**
- Sign-up friction for commenting.
- Moderation delays.
- Harassment without tools to mute/block.
- Comment lost (draft not saved).
- Reply notifications missed.

## Subscriber (newsletter)

Email-engaged audience.

**Pain points:**
- Too-frequent newsletters.
- Unsubscribe hard to find.
- Content not matching signup expectation.
- Email rendering bad in their client.
- Mobile unreadability.

**KPIs:**
- Open rate.
- Click rate.
- Unsubscribe rate.
- Complaint rate.

## Author / writer

The creator. Sometimes staff, sometimes freelance.

**Workflows:**
- Pitch ideas (or assigned).
- Research.
- Draft.
- Self-edit.
- Submit for editorial review.
- Revise per feedback.
- Approve final.
- Promote on social.
- Engage comments (optional).

**Pain points:**
- Editor delays on review.
- Conflicting feedback from multiple editors.
- Asset upload slow / broken.
- Lost work (no autosave, browser crash).
- Schedule published at wrong time.
- No insight into article performance.

**KPIs:**
- Articles published per period.
- Pageviews per article.
- Engagement metrics.
- Social shares.

**Permissions:**
- Draft CRUD on own articles.
- Submit for review.
- Read others' published.
- Profile management.

## Editor

Gatekeeper + coach. Mid-senior role.

**Workflows:**
- Assign stories.
- Review drafts.
- Fact-check.
- Structural + line edit.
- SEO / headline optimization.
- Publish approval.
- Schedule.
- Post-publish amendments (corrections).
- Post-mortems.

**Pain points:**
- Draft queue overwhelming.
- Inline-comment collaboration weak.
- Hard to see pipeline (what's pitched vs in draft vs awaiting edit).
- Style guide inconsistency.
- Scheduling conflicts (50 posts same day).
- Revision history too noisy (every keystroke a revision).

**KPIs:**
- Throughput (articles published).
- Quality metrics (corrections per article, reader complaints).
- Team productivity.

**Permissions:**
- Edit all articles in their scope.
- Schedule + publish.
- Assign.
- Override author revisions.
- Comment moderation approval.

## Managing editor / EIC

Editorial leadership.

**Workflows:**
- Editorial vision.
- Staffing (hire + fire).
- Section oversight.
- Policy decisions (corrections, retractions).
- Legal coordination.
- Reader-facing rep (letters, responses).

**Pain points:**
- Metric visibility across sections.
- Author performance unclear.
- Retention of top contributors.
- Ethical decisions escalated.

## Copy editor / fact-checker

Specialists in accuracy.

**Workflows:**
- Receive article from editor.
- Fact-check every claim.
- Copy edit (grammar, style, consistency).
- Return with markup.
- Track corrections.

**Pain:**
- Styles drift without enforced style guide.
- Cite tracking (link rot).
- Repeat corrections (authors not learning).

## Freelance contributor

External writer; transactional relationship.

**Workflows:**
- Receive assignment or pitch.
- Sign contract.
- Deliver on deadline.
- Invoice.
- Get paid.

**Pain:**
- Contract clarity (rights, kill fee, deadline).
- Slow payment.
- Editor silence between submission + publish.

**Permissions:**
- Draft own articles.
- Limited media library access.

## Photographer / designer / illustrator

Visual contributors.

**Workflows:**
- Brief from editor.
- Deliver assets.
- Credit on published piece.

**Pain:**
- Credit missing.
- Image cropped wrong.
- License terms unclear.

## Multimedia producer

Video / podcast / interactive.

**Workflows:**
- Plan production.
- Produce + edit.
- Prepare distribution (YouTube, Spotify, embed).
- Post-production analytics.

## Social media manager

Amplification role.

**Workflows:**
- Pull stories for distribution.
- Adapt copy per platform (Twitter, LinkedIn, TikTok, Instagram).
- Schedule posts.
- Respond to mentions.
- Community management.
- Analytics.

**Pain:**
- Platform-specific asset requirements.
- Link tracking fragmentation.
- Story performance mystery.

## SEO specialist

Traffic optimization.

**Workflows:**
- Keyword research.
- On-page SEO brief.
- Technical SEO audits.
- Site structure.
- Backlink strategy.
- Analytics.

**Pain:**
- Authors ignore SEO briefs.
- Duplicate content from syndication.
- Core Web Vitals regressions.

## Product + engineering

Platform builders.

**Workflows:**
- Editor tooling.
- Publishing platform.
- Reader UX.
- Ad infrastructure.
- Analytics.

**Pain:**
- Custom-CMS technical debt.
- Editorial asks conflict with reader experience.
- Third-party script accretion slows site.

## Advertising / revenue

Monetization role.

**Workflows:**
- Ad sales (direct + programmatic).
- Placement optimization.
- Yield management.
- Sponsored content deals.
- Sponsorship activation.

**Pain:**
- Ad blockers.
- Brand safety.
- Privacy law (cookies, targeting).
- Transparency tension with editorial.

## Legal

Contracts + risk.

**Workflows:**
- Contract review.
- Pre-publication legal review (libel, privacy).
- DMCA + takedown.
- Defamation response.
- Privacy compliance.

**Pain:**
- Reactive (engaged too late).
- Budget pressure.
- Source protection.

## Ombudsman / public editor

Accountability role (diminishing institution).

**Workflows:**
- Reader complaint intake.
- Editorial accountability review.
- Public correction recommendations.

## Commerce / membership (paid subscriptions)

If monetization beyond ads.

**Workflows:**
- Subscription product management.
- Conversion optimization (paywall strategy).
- Retention.
- Billing + support.

**Pain:**
- Paywall hurts reach.
- Free rider problem.
- Churn.
- Free-to-paid conversion.

## Partners / syndicators

Content distribution.

**Workflows:**
- Content licensing.
- Feed provision.
- Traffic + brand exchange.

## Analytics + data

Measurement.

**Workflows:**
- Dashboard building.
- A/B testing.
- Audience analytics.
- Revenue attribution.

## IT + infrastructure

Operations.

**Workflows:**
- Site reliability.
- Cache invalidation during breaking news.
- Security + DDoS.
- Email delivery.

## Search engines + social platforms

External distributors.

**Touchpoints:**
- Google Search (crawl, index, rank).
- Google News (inclusion, preferences).
- Facebook / Twitter / LinkedIn (shares).
- Apple News (publisher program).
- Flipboard, SmartNews.

**Pain:**
- Algorithm changes kill traffic.
- No direct relationship for leverage.

## Regulators

- **FTC** — advertising disclosure, FTC Act Section 5 (deceptive practices).
- **State AGs** — state consumer protection.
- **EU authorities** — GDPR + DSA (for large platforms).
- **Copyright Office** — DMCA agent registration.
- **FCC** (for broadcast content).

## Plaintiffs (litigation)

- Copyright holders (DMCA).
- Defamation plaintiffs.
- Accessibility (ADA lawsuits).
- Privacy (class actions).

## Stakeholder-driven feature priorities

| If complaint is from... | Then priority is... |
|---|---|
| Readers abandoning | Performance + Core Web Vitals + mobile UX |
| Authors frustrated | Editor UX + autosave + revision UI |
| Editors drowning | Pipeline dashboard + bulk actions + inline review |
| SEO team losing traffic | Canonical URLs + redirects + structured data |
| Newsletter bouncing | Deliverability (SPF/DKIM/DMARC) + List-Unsubscribe |
| Legal stressed | DMCA workflow + corrections log + source protection |
| Revenue team missing targets | Ad inventory + sponsorship CMS + membership conversion |
| IT paged on traffic spike | Cache + CDN + static rendering |

## Anti-pattern: "build the editor for the editor-in-chief, not the reporter"

Most content platforms optimize for the senior editor's workflow (approve, schedule, override). But the person typing in the editor 6 hours a day is a reporter. If the editor UI is slow, bloated, or requires 15 clicks per article, productivity collapses. Editor-in-chief tools are important; they're secondary to the writer's speed.
