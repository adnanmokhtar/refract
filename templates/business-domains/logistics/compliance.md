# Logistics — compliance + legal

Logistics is the intersection of transportation, customs, labor, environmental, and data privacy law. Each layer has its own regulator, paperwork, and enforcement.

## Federal — DOT (Department of Transportation, US)

### FMCSA (Federal Motor Carrier Safety Administration)
- **Hours of Service (HOS)** — 49 CFR Part 395:
  - 11-hour driving limit after 10 consecutive hours off-duty.
  - 14-hour on-duty limit.
  - 30-min break after 8 cumulative hours of driving.
  - 60/70 hour weekly limit (7/8 day rolling).
  - Exemptions: short-haul, agricultural, emergency.
- **ELD mandate** — electronic logging required for HOS recording (since 2017 / 2019 for older devices).
- **CDL (Commercial Driver License)** — required for vehicles >26,001 lbs GVWR or hazmat or 16+ passengers.
- **DVIR** (Driver Vehicle Inspection Report) — pre-trip + post-trip, OOS items must be documented.
- **Drug + Alcohol Testing** — pre-employment, random, post-accident, reasonable suspicion.
- **MCS-150** biennial filing.
- **DOT Number** + USDOT registration.

### Hazardous Materials Regulations (HMR, 49 CFR 100-185)
- Classes: 1 (Explosives), 2 (Gases), 3 (Flammable Liquids), 4 (Flammable Solids), 5 (Oxidizers), 6 (Toxic), 7 (Radioactive), 8 (Corrosive), 9 (Misc Dangerous).
- Shipping papers (hazmat manifest), labels, placards, packaging.
- Driver hazmat endorsement (CDL-H).
- Limited Quantity simplifies for small consumer shipments.
- ORM-D phased out 2021.

## International transport regulations

### Air (IATA Dangerous Goods Regulations)
- Annual update; current edition required.
- Per-air-carrier variations.
- Lithium battery rules tightening (UN 3480/3481/3090/3091).
- Passenger vs cargo aircraft distinctions.
- Forbidden in air entirely (some classes).

### Ocean (IMDG Code)
- International Maritime Dangerous Goods.
- IMDG packaging + container stowage rules.
- Reefer + refrigerated container regs.

### Road (ADR Europe, TDG Canada)
- ADR — European agreement for road dangerous goods.
- TDG (Transportation of Dangerous Goods, Canada).

## Customs + cross-border

### US Customs (CBP)
- **HS / HTSUS classification** required on every import — duty rate hinges on classification.
- **Country of Origin** marking on goods + documentation.
- **De minimis threshold** — Section 321: <$800 US import value duty-free (under threat of repeal 2024).
- **Importer of Record** (IOR) responsibility.
- **CBP Form 7501** entry summary.
- **Customs broker license** required for commercial entries (or self-file with risk).
- **C-TPAT** (Customs-Trade Partnership Against Terrorism) — voluntary, expedited clearance for verified shippers.
- **Forced Labor (UFLPA — Uyghur Forced Labor Prevention Act 2022)** — rebuttable presumption that goods from Xinjiang are made with forced labor; broad supply-chain due diligence.

### EU Customs (Union Customs Code)
- **EORI** number required for all EU operators.
- **VAT collection** at import (or IOSS if <€150).
- **CN22 / CN23** customs declarations for postal.
- **CDS** (Customs Declaration Service, UK).
- **CBAM** (Carbon Border Adjustment Mechanism 2026 full enforcement) — carbon tariffs on imports of carbon-intensive goods.

### Other major
- **GST** (Canada — registration thresholds; collected at import or by seller).
- **VAT in destination** for many countries; threshold-based seller registration.
- **Customs forms per country** — vary widely.

## Incoterms 2020 (ICC)

Allocate cost + risk + clearance responsibility between buyer + seller.

- **EXW** (Ex Works) — buyer takes from seller's premises.
- **FCA** (Free Carrier) — seller delivers to named carrier.
- **FOB** (Free On Board) — seller responsible until on vessel; ocean only.
- **CIF** (Cost, Insurance, Freight) — seller pays freight + insurance to destination port.
- **DAP** (Delivered At Place) — seller delivers to named place; buyer clears customs.
- **DPU** (Delivered at Place Unloaded).
- **DDP** (Delivered Duty Paid) — seller pays everything including duties.
- **DDU** (Delivered Duty Unpaid) — deprecated in 2020 (replaced by DAP); still in common usage.

## Driver privacy

### Location tracking
- US — generally permissible during work hours with notice; off-duty tracking discouraged.
- EU GDPR — purpose limitation; minimum necessary; retention defined.
- California — CCPA + CPRA worker data extension.
- Union jurisdictions — collective bargaining may restrict.

### Biometric capture
- Illinois BIPA — consent required + retention schedule + 5-year purge.
- Texas, Washington — biometric privacy.
- Driver photo / face scan for in-app login = biometric data.

## Privacy

### GDPR (EU)
- Recipient names + addresses = personal data.
- Driver location = personal data.
- Tracking event histories = personal data (linked to recipient).
- Lawful basis: contract (delivery service) typically.
- Data minimization: don't keep tracking history beyond business need.
- DPA with all sub-processors (carriers, address validation services, mapping).

### CCPA / CPRA + state
- Driver data (employee/contractor) per CPRA worker provisions.
- Recipient data per consumer rules.

### Country-specific
- China PIPL — strict cross-border for shipping data.
- Brazil LGPD — recipient data subject rights.
- India DPDP — emerging.

## Labor + employment

### US
- **Fair Labor Standards Act** — minimum wage, overtime, child labor.
- **Independent contractor classification** — gig drivers test (ABC test in CA, AB-5; Prop 22 narrowly carved out app-based; ongoing federal DOL rulemaking).
- **OSHA** workplace safety (warehouse, dock).
- **Workers' compensation** per state.

### EU
- Working Time Directive (max 48h/week unless opt-out).
- Posted Workers Directive (cross-border driver rules).
- Mobility Package — return-to-base obligations + tachograph rules.

### Worker misclassification (gig economy)
- Multiple lawsuits + state laws; reclassification can be retroactive + liability massive.
- Document worker control (or lack thereof).

## Insurance

- **Cargo insurance** — first-party loss/damage during transit.
- **Liability** — third-party harm caused by operations.
- **Auto liability** — fleet-specific.
- **Cyber liability** — ransomware on TMS.
- **Errors & Omissions** — for brokers / 3PLs.
- **Workers' comp** — driver injuries.

## Carrier-specific contractual obligations

### Carrier service agreements
- Tariff schedules + rate cards.
- Service level agreements (on-time %, claim handling).
- Liability limits ("released value" vs "declared value").
- Force majeure clauses.

### NMFC (National Motor Freight Classification)
- Freight class (50-500) based on density, stowability, handling, liability.
- Determines LTL pricing.

## Environmental

- **EPA SmartWay** — voluntary US program for fuel efficiency tracking.
- **CARB** (California Air Resources Board) — emissions, refrigerant.
- **Greenhouse Gas reporting** (SEC climate disclosures, EU CSRD).
- **CBAM** (EU 2026 full).
- **ULEZ / clean air zones** — Europe; vehicle restrictions in city centers.

## Recordkeeping

- **HOS records** — 6 months minimum.
- **DVIR** — 3 months.
- **Drug + alcohol records** — 5 years (some 10).
- **Driver qualification file** — duration of employment + 3 years.
- **Customs records** — 5 years US; varies internationally.
- **Hazmat training** — 3 years.
- **Cargo claims** — per carrier contract + statute of limitations.
- **PII** — per privacy law + business purpose.

## Vehicle + operations

- **DOT inspection** annual + roadside random.
- **Vehicle registration** + apportioned plates (IRP) for interstate.
- **IFTA** (International Fuel Tax Agreement) — quarterly fuel tax filings.
- **UCR** (Unified Carrier Registration) annual.

## Address + sanctions screening

- **OFAC SDN List** — sanctioned parties screen on shipper + recipient.
- **BIS Entity List** (US Commerce Department) — denied parties.
- **EU sanctions list** — Russia, Iran, etc.
- **UK sanctions** — post-Brexit list maintenance.
- Block + alert on hits; do not ship.

## Common compliance gaps in v1

- Driver location tracked off-duty (not just on-route) → privacy violation in EU + CA.
- HOS warnings as suggestions not hard blocks → DOT violation cascade.
- Hazmat shipping enabled without driver hazmat training tracking.
- Address validation NOT screening sanctioned countries / parties.
- International shipments without HS code → customs hold + delays.
- Customer service can issue refund on non-arrived shipment without claim filed → write-off without recovery.
- Driver app collects biometric (face login) without BIPA consent in IL.
- Sub-processor (carrier API gateway) usage without DPA in customer's BAA.
- No record of consent for SMS notifications to recipients (TCPA).
- Photo POD includes faces of bystanders (privacy + GDPR).
- Driver background check expired but driver still active.
- Vehicle insurance lapsed but route assigned.
- Manifest not closed → carrier rebills + relationship damage.
- Webhook secrets stored in plaintext config.
- Tracking history retained indefinitely (storage cost + privacy violation).
- Cross-border without IOR registered → customs delays + customer-pays-duty surprise.
- Lithium battery shipment without IATA-compliant labeling and acknowledgment.
- Children's items shipped without CPSC compliance documentation (consignee may demand).
- DDP advertised but not actually collected → customs returns or angry customer.
- No process for sanctioned-recipient block → US/EU sanctions violation, fine + prosecution.
- Driver photo retention forever → BIPA + GDPR violations.
