# Glossary — Acme Orders

The words the product uses. Code, routes, translation keys and event names use the term in the
first column; the words in the last column are not used anywhere.

| Term | Meaning | Not |
|---|---|---|
| customer | an account that places orders | client, buyer, shopper |
| order | a confirmed purchase of one or more items | purchase, sale |
| deleted | an order a staff member removed (soft delete, kept 90 days) | removed, erased, trashed |
| archived | an order hidden from the default lists, still reportable | hidden, stashed |
| refund | money returned against a paid order | chargeback (that is the card network's word) |
