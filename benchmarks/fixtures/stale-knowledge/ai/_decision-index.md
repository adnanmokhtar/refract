## How to use

- Before an architectural change, find the area here and read that ADR first.
- A proposal that contradicts an accepted ADR either follows it or writes a superseding ADR.
- Superseded rows stay for context; cite the successor.

## How this is maintained

- The curator regenerates this file after every promoted decision.
- The ADR files are the source of truth; this is a derived index.
